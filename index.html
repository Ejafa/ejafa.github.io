TreemapMix: Dirichlet-Controlled Multi-Image Augmentation for Probability and Ordinal Supervision ↗

Ejafa Bassam, Konstantin Garbers, Yingsheng Geng, Dalton Jens, Jiaxu Qian, Guyue Liu

Can multi-image composition provide both probability and ordinal supervision? I conceived and led TreemapMix, using area-proportional treemap layouts and cyclic views to turn image composition into richer learning signals.

My contribution +

Extended my work on ordinal supervision from teacher-derived rankings to supervision generated directly from multi-image composition. Developed a Dirichlet-controlled K-way augmentation, combining soft cross-entropy with a Plackett-Luce objective, and built the mathematical formulation, implementation, and large-scale experimental framework.

02NeurIPS
2025
Knowledge distillationLearning objectives

PLD: A Choice-Theoretic List-Wise Knowledge Distillation ↗

Ejafa Bassam, Dawei Zhu, Kaigui Bian

Does a student need to match a teacher’s exact probability distribution? I explored class ranking as an alternative supervisory signal, developing a choice-theoretic Plackett-Luce list-wise objective for knowledge distillation.

My contribution +

Developed a ranking-based objective that integrates with CE+KL distillation pipelines and demonstrated ranking-based matching as an effective alternative to KL minimization. My M.Sc. thesis extended this direction to multi-teacher distillation, transferring the distinct class rankings of multiple teachers.

DecentFL: A Decentralized Federated Learning System with Multiple Aggregators and Evaluators ↗

Khushal Haider Syed, Ruben Poghosyan, Hongjian Shi, Ruhui Ma, Yuan Luo, Ejafa Bassam, Ziang Zhou, Zhongle Qu, Haibing Guan

Experimental implementation and benchmarking for blockchain-coordinated decentralized training, evaluating CNN and ResNet-18 models across 100 non-IID clients.

My contribution +

Implemented pipelines for FedAvg, FedProx, FedDBE, and GPFL under Dirichlet and pathological label-skew settings. Conducted multi-seed experiments over 100 to 200 global rounds to evaluate convergence behavior.
